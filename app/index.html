About this analysis

Data

A random ~6.5% sample of the Yelp Open Dataset: reviews of restaurants in 13 US states and Alberta, cleaned in PostgreSQL.

Method

Complaint topics were found with keyword lists in Python. Reviews of hotels, bars and other mixed businesses were kept only when the text is about dining ( reviews). Star cost comes from a linear regression that splits the blame when a review mentions several complaints.

Read with care

Keyword tagging is approximate: about 19% of negative reviews match no topic. The numbers show what is associated with low ratings, not what causes them.
